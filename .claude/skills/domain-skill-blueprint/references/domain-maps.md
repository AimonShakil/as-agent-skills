# Starter value-stream maps

Checklists for Step 1. Adapt to the user's context — add, drop or rename streams as the business requires. For domains not listed, derive streams from APQC PCF (Level 1–2 categories) and the domain's dominant professional body.

## Contents
- Human Resources (HR)
- Finance & Accounting
- Sales
- Marketing
- Customer Support / Service
- Procurement & Supply Chain
- Legal
- IT / Internal Ops

## Human Resources (HR)
Frameworks: APQC PCF 7.0 "Develop and Manage Human Capital", SHRM BoCK, CIPD Profession Map.
- **Workforce planning** — headcount planning, org design, skills/role architecture
- **Talent acquisition (recruit-to-hire)** — requisition, JD, sourcing, screening, interviewing, selection, offer
- **Onboarding** — pre-boarding, paperwork/compliance (I-9 etc.), orientation, 30-60-90 plans
- **Performance & development** — goals/OKRs, reviews, feedback, learning paths, career frameworks
- **Total rewards** — compensation benchmarking, pay bands, bonus/merit cycles, benefits enrollment & questions
- **Employee relations & compliance** — policy, handbook, investigations, disciplinary process, labor-law compliance
- **Employee experience & engagement** — surveys, recognition, DEI, wellbeing, HR helpdesk
- **Offboarding (retire)** — exits, exit interviews, final pay, knowledge transfer
- **People analytics & HR ops** — HRIS data, reporting, attrition, headcount, audits
Common regulations: GDPR/CCPA (employee data), EEOC/Title VII, ADA, FLSA, FMLA, local labor codes, pay-transparency laws.
Never fully delegate: hiring/firing decisions, pay decisions, investigation findings.

## Finance & Accounting
Frameworks: APQC PCF "Manage Financial Resources", COSO, IFRS/GAAP, SOX.
Streams: record-to-report (close, reconciliations, journals), procure-to-pay (AP), order-to-cash (AR, collections), FP&A (budget, forecast, variance), treasury, tax, audit & controls, expense management.

## Sales
Frameworks: MEDDICC/MEDDPICC, Winning by Design bowtie, Challenger.
Streams: territory & account planning, prospecting & outreach, discovery & qualification, solutioning & proposals, negotiation & closing, forecasting & pipeline hygiene, handoff to customer success, sales enablement.

## Marketing
Frameworks: AARRR funnel, STP, 4Ps/7Ps.
Streams: market & audience research, positioning & messaging, content production, campaign planning & execution, demand gen/lead management, brand & creative review, analytics & attribution, events/PR.

## Customer Support / Service
Frameworks: ITIL service operation, KCS (Knowledge-Centered Service), COPC.
Streams: intake & triage, resolution & troubleshooting, escalation, knowledge management, QA & coaching, VoC/feedback analysis, SLA/workforce management.

## Procurement & Supply Chain
Frameworks: SCOR (Plan/Source/Make/Deliver/Return/Enable), APQC.
Streams: demand planning, sourcing & RFx, supplier onboarding & risk, contracting, purchase-to-pay, inventory & logistics, supplier performance, returns.

## Legal
Frameworks: CLOC core competencies.
Streams: contract lifecycle (draft, review, negotiate, sign, obligations), legal intake & triage, compliance & regulatory monitoring, privacy, IP, disputes & litigation support, corporate governance, policy management.

## IT / Internal Ops
Frameworks: ITIL 4, COBIT.
Streams: service desk (request/incident), change & release, asset & access management, problem management, security operations, vendor/SaaS management, knowledge base.
