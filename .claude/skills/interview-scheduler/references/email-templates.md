# Email templates

Adapt these to the coordinator's tone and company, but keep the content. Write `body` as plain text (no Markdown). Use `htmlBody` only if the coordinator wants formatting. Set `includeSignature: true` so the coordinator's Gmail signature is added.

Always state the time zone next to every time, e.g. "Tue 20 Oct 2026, 14:00–15:00 (GMT+5, Asia/Karachi)".

---

## 1. Availability request (to one panelist)

**Subject:** Availability for <Candidate Name>'s <Round> interview (<Role>)

Hi <Panelist first name>,

We're scheduling a <duration>-minute <round> interview with <Candidate Name> for the <Role> role, sometime between <window start> and <window end>.

Could you reply with two or three windows that work for you, including your time zone? For example: "Tue 20 Oct 14:00–17:00 ET, Wed 21 Oct 10:00–12:00 ET".

Thanks,

---

## 2. Candidate invitation

**Subject:** Your <Round> interview for <Role> at <Company> — <Day, DD Mon>

Hi <Candidate first name>,

Thank you for your interest in the <Role> role. We'd like to confirm your <round> interview:

When: <Day, DD Mon YYYY>, <HH:MM–HH:MM> <candidate TZ> (<coordinator-TZ time> <coordinator TZ>)
Duration: <N> minutes
Where: Zoom: <join URL>
Meeting ID: <id>   Passcode: <passcode>

You'll be meeting:
- <Panelist name>, <title if known>
- ...

<What to prepare: e.g. "Please have a code editor ready; we'll work through a problem together." Omit this if nothing was specified. Don't invent requirements.>

If this time no longer works, just reply to this email and we'll find another.

Best regards,

(Never include the evaluation sheet link, panelists' email addresses or internal notes.)

---

## 3. Panelist briefing (to all panelists)

**Subject:** Interview panel: <Candidate Name> — <Role> (<Round>), <Day, DD Mon HH:MM TZ>

Hi all,

You're on the panel for <Candidate Name>'s <round> interview for <Role>.

When: <Day, DD Mon YYYY>, <HH:MM–HH:MM> <coordinator TZ>
Zoom: <join URL> (ID <id>, passcode <passcode>)
Evaluation sheet: <sheet URL> (you have edit access)
Candidate profile/resume: <link, if provided>

Focus areas:
- <Panelist>: <focus>
- ...

Please fill in your section of the evaluation sheet independently (before talking with the other panelists) and submit it by <deadline, default: within 24 hours after the interview>.

Thanks,

---

## 4. Updated time (reschedule)

**Subject:** Updated time: <original subject>

Start with the change, e.g.: "Your interview has moved from <old time TZ> to <new time TZ>. The previous time is cancelled." Then repeat the full details block from the original template.
